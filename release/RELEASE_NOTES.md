Holtburger beta includes the 3D client and the terminal client (CLI).

## In this beta

- **3D client, alerts:** Alerting entities now show a star crest on their nameplate and a glowing beacon in the world, so threats are easier to spot at a distance.
- **3D client, animation:** Characters can now move and blend upper-body animation while switching stances and performing manual gestures.

## Known issues

- Windows and macOS may warn when opening the app because it is not signed by a verified publisher. The installation guides below explain how to open it.
- Borderless window sizing is best effort and may not match the requested size exactly.
- The runtime console keeps only the most recent messages, and its history is cleared when the client restarts.

## Updating

Quit Holtburger before installing an update. Beta and nightly builds share the same installation, so installing either replaces the other. No other action is needed for this release.
