Holtburger beta includes the 3D client and the terminal client (CLI).

## In this beta

- **3D client, spells:** PgUp and PgDn now cycle through non-empty spell tabs, with configurable shortcuts and a clearer spell bar showing the next empty tab.
- **3D client and CLI, chat commands:** Server commands now go through consistently, and a leading space sends text as speech instead of running a local command.
- **3D client and CLI, death and portals:** Your character's death animation now carries through the portal handoff.

## Known issues

- Windows and macOS may warn when opening the app because it is not signed by a verified publisher. The installation guides below explain how to open it.
- Borderless window sizing is best effort and may not match the requested size exactly.

## Updating

Quit Holtburger before installing an update. Saved profiles and settings are retained. Beta and nightly builds share the same installation; installing either replaces the other.
