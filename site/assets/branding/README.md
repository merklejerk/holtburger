# Site branding

The [copper wordmark](wordmark.webp) is the approved illustrated site title,
combining the original copper lettering with the dimensional portal from an
imagegen exploration in place of the lowercase “o”. Its transparent background
lets the header's parchment show through. Preserve its lettering, copper
highlights, and portal shading.

The site uses a 1200 × 400 transparent WebP at quality 85. The 2172 × 724
composited source lives in the
[media repository](https://github.com/merklejerk/holtburger-media)
as `branding/wordmark-source-v2.png`, alongside the original copper artwork at
`branding/wordmark-source-v1.png`. Export directly from the composited source
when changing the web asset; avoid recompressing the WebP.

Asset preparation copies it to the generated `static/assets/branding/` directory.
The portal emblem and favicon remain shared assets in the repository-root
`assets/branding/` directory.
