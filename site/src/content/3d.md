## Your HUD at a glance

<Media id="hud-showcase" />

| Component                                                                     | Where                           | What it does                                                                              |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Character HUD](/holtburger/3d/character/)                                    | Top left                        | Your health, stamina and mana, plus connection status.                                    |
| [Party HUD](/holtburger/3d/community/#form-a-party)                           | Left, below your vitals         | Teammates’ vitals at a glance; select a member directly.                                  |
| [Selected entity](/holtburger/3d/interactions/#use-the-selected-entity-hud)   | Top center                      | The selected name, health, inspection and relevant actions.                               |
| [Offscreen selection arrow](/holtburger/3d/combat/#keep-track-of-your-target) | Right edge in this view         | Points toward your selected entity when it is outside the viewport.                       |
| [Buffs & debuffs tray](/holtburger/3d/spells/#read-your-current-effects)      | Left, above the party HUD       | Opens your beneficial or harmful enchantments; icons appear as effects become active.     |
| [Minimap & waypoints](/holtburger/3d/maps-travel/)                            | Top right                       | Terrain and nearby entities, coordinates, and direction and distance to your destination. |
| [Jump power](/holtburger/3d/movement-camera/)                                 | Center                          | Your charged jump power, or the power chosen for a precise jump.                          |
| [Spell bar](/holtburger/3d/spells/)                                           | Below center                    | Tabbed spell shortcuts; melee and missile stances show attack controls instead.           |
| [Chat](/holtburger/3d/chat/)                                                  | Bottom left                     | Messages, commands and clickable links, with click-through history when inactive.         |
| [Action bars](/holtburger/3d/action-bars/)                                    | Bottom center                   | Shortcuts for items and equipment loadouts.                                               |
| Notifications                                                                 | Above the action bars           | Brief feedback on actions and errors.                                                     |
| FPS counter                                                                   | Top, beside the selected entity | Frame rate; toggle it with `/fps`.                                                        |
| [Toolbar](/holtburger/3d/interface/)                                          | Bottom right                    | Open your panels and settings.                                                            |

Arrange these components in [Layout & theming](/holtburger/3d/interface/), or see [what changes from retail](/holtburger/coming-from-retail/).
