## Keep your attention on the game

Keep target changes quick with [Smart Tab](/holtburger/3d/combat/). For awkward leaps, [precise jumping](/holtburger/3d/movement-camera/) lets you choose power and inspect the path before committing.

A bold outline makes your selection easy to spot. A separate combat reticle marks the target of your active attack or cast.

<Media id="smart-tab" />

## Keep attack controls within reach

The [combat HUD](/holtburger/3d/combat/#your-attack-controls) groups power or accuracy, attack height and attack engagement into one compact control. Keep your target in view as you adjust the attack.

<Media id="combat-controls" />

## Stay mobile

[Run-casting and archery](/holtburger/3d/combat/#fight-on-the-move) let you keep moving through windups using your normal controls. Keep your distance or reposition without learning sliding key combinations.

<Media id="mobile-combat" />

## Read the route ahead

The [minimap](/holtburger/3d/maps-travel/) shows terrain, buildings, impassable areas and elevation, plus dungeon layouts indoors. Breadcrumbs help you retrace your steps. Set a waypoint and follow its direction and distance as you travel.

<Media id="minimap" />

## Prepare your next move

[Find spells](/holtburger/3d/spells/) by name, school, element, level and targeting. Arrange spell tabs and action bars. Save selected equipment slots as a [loadout](/holtburger/3d/equipment-loadouts/) rather than changing every item individually.

<Media id="spells" />

<Media id="loadouts" />

## Find what you are looking for

Use the [Nearby list](/holtburger/3d/nearby/) to search for a particular creature or object. Set a name alert to catch encounters you would otherwise miss.

## Take a closer look

[Inspect a creature, player or item](/holtburger/3d/inspecting/) without tying up your selection. Keep its appraisal open while changing targets, and rotate creature and player models for a closer look.

## Make the interface yours

[Move and resize the HUD](/holtburger/3d/interface/), adjust UI and text scale independently, and customize bindings.

<Media id="hud" />

## Before you connect

You need your own game data and an account on an ACE server. [Start playing](/holtburger/start/) or read the [retail migration guide](/holtburger/coming-from-retail/).

Both clients are in active development. h3d has no Decal or plugin compatibility; GDLE support is unverified.
