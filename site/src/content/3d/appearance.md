## Barbershop

Interact with your server’s barber service to open **Barbershop**. Changes preview live on your character in the world as you adjust them; choose **Apply** to save your new look. Available choices follow your character’s heritage.

<Media id="barber" />

## Show or hide your helmet and cloak

Open **Inventory** and use the helmet and cloak visibility toggles beneath the [equipment strip](/holtburger/3d/inventory/#equipment-at-a-glance). Hide either to show your character’s appearance while keeping the gear equipped.
