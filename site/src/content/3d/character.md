## Review your character

Open the character sheet for attributes, vitals, skills, experience and luminance. Expand a stat for its detail and available improvement actions.

Skill sections distinguish trained and specialized skills. Check the displayed costs before spending experience; an improvement button can purchase immediately. Training a new skill has a separate confirmation.

## Improve a stat or train a skill

Choose the improvement beside the stat you want to raise. Read unavailable-action explanations when you cannot buy the next improvement—for example, when you lack the required currency or have reached a cap.

To train a skill, choose **Train**, review the cost, and confirm. Check the skill's new state afterward.

## Understand defenses

The **Defenses** view groups your defensive values for easier inspection. Resistance values can show a reduction or a vulnerability, but they are only part of what determines damage; armor and the attack also matter.

Use [Enchantments](/holtburger/3d/enchantments/) to see which effects are currently helping or hurting those stats.

## Choose a title

Use the title control to choose from the titles your character has earned. Title changes are separate from spending experience or training skills.
