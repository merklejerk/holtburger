## Ready your weapon

Equip your weapon or caster, then press **Backquote (`)** to switch between peace and combat. Your equipped weapon determines the available combat stance and controls.

Choose your target with [Smart Tab](/holtburger/3d/selection-interaction/), then start the attack when you are ready.

## Start or stop attacking

Use **Start attack** on the combat bar once you have chosen your target. The control becomes **Cancel attack** while attacking. **Escape** can cancel the current attack.

For spells, enter magic stance and [cast from the spell list or spell bar](/holtburger/3d/spells/).

## Adjust the attack

| Control             | What it changes                     |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Power               | Your chosen melee attack power      |
| Accuracy            | Your chosen missile accuracy        |
| High / Medium / Low | Attack height                       |
| Preset buttons      | Quick choices for power or accuracy |

Drag the gauge to choose a value between presets. Missile combat also shows ammunition supply. Check that you have the right ammunition before starting a fight.

Number-key actions depend on whether you are using combat controls, spells, or a focused action bar. Use the hints shown on the relevant control, or customize them under **Settings → Input**.

## Between fights

Use [loadouts](/holtburger/3d/equipment-loadouts/) for planned gear changes, and check [enchantments](/holtburger/3d/enchantments/) when a buff or debuff changes your effective stats.
