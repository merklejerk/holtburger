## Find housing

Open **World** and choose a house type in **Map visibility → Housing** to search. Inspect the results on the map and use them to plan your visit.

The **Housing** tab shows information about your current home when available.

## Inspect a house crystal

Interact with the house crystal to see ownership, eligibility, and purchase or maintenance requirements.

Prepare the requested items in the payment area. **Auto-fill** helps choose items you carry for a requirement; review the prepared amounts before submitting the purchase or maintenance payment.

## Manage access

Under **Access & guests**, set public entry, allegiance entry and allegiance storage access as appropriate. Add guests by character name, then decide which guests should have storage access.

Entry and storage permissions are separate choices. Review both when opening your house to other people.

You can remove individual guests, remove all guests, or revoke storage access. Booting an occupant removes them from the house without removing their guest permission.

## Decorations and leaving

Use **Decorations** to show or hide empty hooks. **Advanced** contains **Abandon home…**; review that action carefully before giving up your home.
