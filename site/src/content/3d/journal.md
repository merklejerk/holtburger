## Find a contract

The Journal shortcut pulses when you acquire a new contract. Open **Journal** and search by contract name. Use the status filters to narrow the list to available, in-progress, completed, or waiting-to-repeat contracts. **All** clears the status restriction.

Select an entry to read its status, progress and available details. Timed contracts show remaining time; repeatable contracts can show when they become available again.

## Plan the trip

Check the contract's contact location and quest area. Enable **Contracts** on the [world map](/holtburger/3d/maps-travel/) to see the available contract destinations alongside your other map layers.

</IllustratedProse>

## Clean up the list

Use **Remove from journal** on an entry you no longer want to track. If an entry has unresolved or missing information, keep its identifier and status when reporting the problem through [Help](/holtburger/3d/help/).
