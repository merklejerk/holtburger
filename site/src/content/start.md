Choose your platform for installation instructions:

## Connect and enter the world

1. Have your AC game files ready. [Use your existing DATs or download them](/holtburger/startup/settings/).
2. In the launcher, choose **Import from TreeStats** and add your server. Holtburger is developed against **ACE**; **GDLE compatibility is unverified**.
3. Enter your account credentials and press **Play**.
4. Choose your DAT folder if prompted, then select your character and enter the world.

[Servers & accounts](/holtburger/startup/servers/) covers saved profiles and manual server entry. Once connected, follow [your first session](/holtburger/start/first-session/) or explore the [3D tour](/holtburger/3d/).
