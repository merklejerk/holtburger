## Enter with an existing character

Select a character in the roster, then use the enter-world button or press **Enter**. Selecting a row by itself does not enter the game.

The preview can show your character's last observed appearance. A character you have not played through Holtburger yet may not have an appearance preview; you can still enter normally.

<Media id="characters" />

## Create a character

Choose **Create** when the server has an available character slot. Work through appearance, **Skills & Stats**, and the final review.

Choose your heritage and appearance, assign your starting attributes and skills, and check the result before pressing **Create character**. Starter clothing can be randomized separately from your appearance.

After the server accepts creation, the new character is selected in the roster. Enter the world when you are ready.

<Media id="character-creation" />

## Delete or restore

Use the delete action beside a character and review the confirmation. A character awaiting deletion has a restore action instead. Availability depends on the character's deletion state on your server.

## Change your appearance later

Use your server's barber service to open **Barbershop**. Adjust the available appearance choices, then choose **Apply** to submit the changes or **Cancel** to leave them alone. The choices depend on your character's heritage.
