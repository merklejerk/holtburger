## Choose a server

Holtburger connects to community-run servers. Import one from **TreeStats** in the launcher, then follow that server’s instructions to create an account. Choose an ACE server; GDLE compatibility is unverified.

## Get connected

The [installation guide](/holtburger/start/) takes you through downloading Holtburger and connecting. [Game data & launch settings](/holtburger/startup/settings/) includes the DAT download if you need game files.

## Learn the game

The [AC Community Wiki](https://asheron.info/wiki/index.php/ACC_Wiki_Home) covers skills, quests, equipment and places. Your server’s community is the best place for advice specific to its world.

[Create your character](/holtburger/startup/creation/), then follow [your first session](/holtburger/start/first-session/) for Holtburger’s controls and conveniences.
