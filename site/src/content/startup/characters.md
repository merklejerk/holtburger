## Choose your character

Select your character and press **Enter** or use the enter-world button. The roster preview uses the appearance last seen through Holtburger.

<Media id="character-roster" detail />

For a new character, open [character creation](/holtburger/startup/creation/). Delete and restore actions are beside the roster entries; deletion asks you to confirm the character name.
