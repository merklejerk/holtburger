## Switch characters

Open the **System menu** and choose **Return to main menu**. This logs out your character and returns to character selection on the same connection.

## Switch accounts or servers

Choose **Disconnect**, then return to the launcher. Select another server or account profile and press **Play**.

**Quit application** closes the client.
