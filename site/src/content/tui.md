## A client you can play

The dashboard brings together nearby entities, inventory, equipment, spells, character information, trade and fellowship. Chat and context panes keep commands and item details close at hand.

## A client you can script

Write JavaScript against structured state, events and actions. One script runs at a time; starting another replaces it. The bundled fighter and mage are fully functional combat bots provided as examples to study and adapt when building your own scripts.

The [scripting guide](/holtburger/tui/scripting/) covers writing and running scripts, the API, persistence and HTTP integrations.

## Set expectations

The TUI uses simplified movement and physics. Supervise scripts through difficult terrain. Trade and buff services need scripts tailored to those tasks.

## Get connected

Release bundles include prepared content and scripts, so you can connect straight from the terminal. Supply your connection details at launch.

[Download the terminal client](/holtburger/tui/connect/) for your platform.
